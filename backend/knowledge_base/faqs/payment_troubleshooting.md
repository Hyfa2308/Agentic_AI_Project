# Payment Troubleshooting Guide

## Resolving Failed Transactions
If your payment fails during checkout:
1. Verify that your credit/debit card billing zip code matches your bank record.
2. Ensure your card supports international online payments and has sufficient balance.
3. Do NOT click the submit payment button repeatedly to avoid duplicate bank authorization holds.
4. Try using an alternate payment method or card.

## Duplicate Charges & Authorization Holds
Pending holds shown twice on your bank statement for a single order are temporary verification holds placed by card issuers.
- Duplicate authorization holds auto-release within 3-5 business days depending on your issuing bank.
- If a double charge posts as settled transactions, contact support immediately with your transaction IDs for refund processing.
