# Account Recovery & Sign-In Help

## Resetting Your Password
If you cannot sign in or have forgotten your account password:
1. Navigate to the login page and click the "Forgot Password" link.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Check your email inbox for a secure password reset link (valid for 30 minutes).
4. Click the link and enter a new password containing at least 8 characters, one number, and one special symbol.

## Locked Account Protocol
Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts to prevent brute force attacks.
- Locked accounts automatically unlock after 30 minutes.
- If your account remains locked, submit a support ticket with your registered email and Customer ID for manual identity verification.
