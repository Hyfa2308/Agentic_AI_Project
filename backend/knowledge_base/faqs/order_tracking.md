# Order Tracking & Delivery Support

## Tracking Your Order
Once an order has shipped:
1. You will receive an automated email containing your tracking carrier link and Tracking ID.
2. Log into your AssistIQ Portal account and visit the Orders tab to check live status updates.
3. Express shipping typically takes 2-3 business days; standard ground shipping takes 5-7 business days.

## Delayed or Damaged Shipments
If your order has not arrived past the estimated delivery date or arrives damaged:
- Submit a ticket via the Support Portal specifying your Order ID and description.
- Photos of damaged packaging must be attached for insurance claim processing.
