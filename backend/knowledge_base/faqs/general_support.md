# General Support FAQ

## Customer Support Hours
AssistIQ AI Assistant is available 24/7/365 to resolve queries automatically.
Human Support Operations operate:
- Monday through Friday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM EST
- Saturday & Sunday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST

## Support Channels
AssistIQ supports Web Chat and the Web Support Portal. All tickets submitted via either channel are centrally stored in PostgreSQL with full audit tracking.
