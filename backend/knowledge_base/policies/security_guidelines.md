# Security & Account Compromise Protocol

## Reporting Unauthorized Access
If you suspect unauthorized activity or believe your account has been compromised:
1. Immediately change your account password and revoke active sessions under Security Settings.
2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via authenticator app.
3. Submit an urgent support ticket. Security alerts automatically trigger CRITICAL priority escalation to our Security Operations Center.

## Data Privacy & Protection
AssistIQ enforces SOC-2 Type II standards, AES-256 encryption at rest, and TLS 1.3 in transit. Customer payment credentials are never stored directly on AssistIQ servers.
