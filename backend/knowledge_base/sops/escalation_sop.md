# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) – Customer Escalations

## 1. Scope and Objective
This SOP defines the guidelines for escalating customer support issues to Tier-2 human support agents.

## 2. Mandatory Escalation Conditions
An issue MUST be escalated to a human agent when:
- The customer expresses extreme anger or threatens legal/regulatory action.
- The transaction involves billing errors, failed payments over $100, or unauthorized charges.
- The AI confidence score for a technical issue is below 60%.
- A cancellation request is submitted for an active enterprise contract.

## 3. Human Agent Protocol
1. Review the AI-generated ticket summary, customer sentiment score, and retrieved knowledge context.
2. Acknowledge customer frustration empathetically within 15 minutes.
3. Validate payment details directly in the billing dashboard before issuing refunds or adjustments.
4. Mark ticket as "resolved" and submit feedback upon customer confirmation.
